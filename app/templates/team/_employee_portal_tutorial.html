Step 1 of 7

Five tabs get you everywhere.

On your phone, Home, Schedule, Hours and Requests are one tap away at the bottom. More has your Inbox (announcements, updates and documents), Profile, Policies, Ops tools and Sign out. On a computer the same pages sit in the left menu.

Step 2 of 7

Start with the status card.

The top card on Home shows whether Clockify has you on the clock, when today's shift starts, or on a day off, when your next shift is.

Step 3 of 7

Check "Needs you".

"Needs you" lists only real to-dos: a policy to sign, a missing phone number or emergency contact, or a timecard day a manager flagged. It's empty when you're caught up.

Step 4 of 7

Hours come from Clockify.

The Last week and This week tiles show your Clockify hours, next to what you were scheduled. Open Hours for the day-by-day list, and tap Ask for help if something looks off.

Step 5 of 7

See your week and your requests.

The week strip marks the days you work (orange dot) and approved time off (blue dot). My requests shows your latest time-off and supply requests and where they stand. The newest announcement sits under Latest, with a dot until you've read it; the rest are in your Inbox.

Step 6 of 7

Finish your profile.

Add your phone and emergency contact and sign your policies (More, then Profile or Policies). That's how managers reach you. If your hours are blank, ask a manager to connect Clockify.

Step 7 of 7

Ask for help from any page.

Tap Ask for help at the top of a page, or More, then Get help, whenever something looks wrong or you're stuck. It tells your manager what page you were on and what you were trying to do.
