Degen Team SMS Privacy Policy

Degen Collectibles LLC · Updated September 14, 2026

Who collects consent

Degen Collectibles LLC collects and manages SMS consent for its Degen Team internal operational alerts program. This policy applies to the program's mobile numbers, SMS preferences, consent records, and messaging records.

Information and purpose

We use your saved mobile number and consent preference to send the informational account notifications you requested about schedules, shifts, and team operations. We record the consenting user, time, disclosure version, saved phone binding, and request metadata for consent auditing. Delivery records and opt-out records support delivery troubleshooting and honoring your preferences.

No marketing sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. SMS opt-in data and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Access is limited to authorized Degen Collectibles LLC personnel and service providers that operate the portal and messaging service, such as Twilio, for those operational purposes. This limited service-provider access does not authorize use of your information or consent for their marketing.

Your choices

SMS is optional. Providing a number alone does not subscribe you. Reply STOP or use Unsubscribe from texts in My Profile to withdraw consent. Opt-out and audit records are retained to honor your preferences and document consent; unsubscribing does not erase those records. App access and portal notifications remain available without texts.

Contact and program terms

For SMS privacy questions or help, contact info@degencollectibles.com. See the SMS Terms of Service and enrollment guide.
