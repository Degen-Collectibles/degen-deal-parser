Degen Team SMS Consent and Terms

Consent version: September 8, 2026

Degen Collectibles offers optional internal operational text alerts to owners and managers who separately choose to subscribe in the Degen Team portal. SMS is not required for an account, employment, or app access.

No marketing texts: This SMS program is not used for promotional campaigns, customer outreach, or public marketing.

How Owners and Managers Opt In

Sign in to My Profile and save your phone number under Your info. Saving a phone number does not subscribe you. In the separate Optional text alerts section, read the SMS terms, select the unchecked opt-in box, and choose Save text preference. Leaving the box unchecked makes no subscription change.

Consent is a separate choice from onboarding, profile updates, and other agreements. The app records who opted in, when, the wording version, and the saved phone record. Changing your phone requires a new opt-in. The same portal features remain available if you decline or unsubscribe.

Send me Degen Collectibles operational text alerts about schedules, shifts, and team operations. Optional; app access does not require consent. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

Message Types

  • Schedule, shift, and team operations notices for subscribed owners and managers.
  • Short alerts link to the authenticated portal for details.

This pilot does not send employee invitations, password resets, two-factor authentication, marketing, or customer outreach by SMS.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies with operational activity. There is no fixed number of alerts per month. SMS is an optional additional channel; portal notifications remain available without subscribing.

Opt Out and Help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from this SMS program. You can also choose Unsubscribe from texts in My Profile. Declining or withdrawing consent does not affect app access. Reply HELP for help, or email info@degencollectibles.com for SMS support.

Rates and Carrier Notice

Message and data rates may apply. Message delivery is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Mobile numbers and consent records are used to operate the internal SMS program and honor recipient preferences. Mobile information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Access is limited to authorized Degen Collectibles personnel and service providers that operate the team portal and SMS delivery systems.
