Degen Team SMS enrollment

Degen Collectibles LLC · Updated September 14, 2026

Degen Collectibles LLC operates Degen Team, an internal portal. Its optional SMS program sends recurring informational account notifications about schedules, shifts, and team operations to subscribed owners and managers.

SMS is optional. Employment, purchases, account creation, and app access do not require SMS consent. Portal notifications remain available without texts. No marketing, customer outreach, employee invitations, password resets, or two-factor authentication messages are sent in this pilot.

Actual enrollment workflow

  1. Sign in to Degen Team → My Profile as an owner or manager.
  2. Save a mobile number in Your info. Saving the number alone does not subscribe you.
  3. Find the separate Optional text alerts section. Read the disclosure below and the directly linked SMS Terms of Service and SMS Privacy Policy.
  4. Check the initially unchecked opt-in box, then choose Save text preference. This separate action records consent. Leaving it unchecked does not subscribe you.

Disclosure shown beside the opt-in checkbox

By checking this box and choosing Save text preference, I agree to receive recurring informational SMS account notifications from Degen Collectibles LLC at my saved mobile number about schedules, shifts, and team operations. Consent is optional and is not a condition of employment, purchase, or app access. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for help.

This public guide describes the authenticated enrollment flow; it does not enroll anyone. The actual checkbox and save action are available inside My Profile. Consent is recorded with the user, time, disclosure version, and saved phone binding. A phone or consent-version change requires a new opt-in.

Unsubscribe and help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe or use Unsubscribe from texts in My Profile. Reply HELP for help or contact info@degencollectibles.com. After a STOP, replying START only clears the carrier block; return to My Profile to give fresh consent. App access remains available.
