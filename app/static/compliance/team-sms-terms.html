Degen Team SMS Terms of Service

Degen Collectibles LLC · Updated September 14, 2026

SMS program and operator

Degen Collectibles LLC operates the Degen Team operational alerts program and collects consent. These terms apply specifically to this internal SMS program.

By checking the separate SMS opt-in box and selecting Save text preference in My Profile, you agree to receive recurring informational SMS account notifications from Degen Collectibles LLC at your saved mobile number about schedules, shifts, and team operations. The pilot is limited to owners and managers. Messages may link to the authenticated portal for details.

Voluntary enrollment

Providing a phone number alone does not authorize texts. Consent is optional, separate from other agreements, and not a condition of employment, purchase, or app access. Portal notifications remain available without subscribing. This program does not send marketing, customer outreach, employee invitations, password resets, or two-factor authentication messages.

Frequency, rates, and delivery

Message frequency varies with operational activity. Message and data rates may apply. Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed.

STOP, HELP, and support

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or choose Unsubscribe from texts in My Profile. Reply HELP for help. For additional assistance, contact info@degencollectibles.com. Following STOP, clear the messaging block with START and then provide fresh consent in My Profile before operational messages resume.

Privacy and related links

The SMS Privacy Policy explains how this program handles mobile numbers and consent records. Review these SMS Terms of Service and the enrollment guide at any time. Changed phone details or updated consent wording require a new opt-in.
